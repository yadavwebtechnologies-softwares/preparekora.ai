About PrepareKora

PrepareKora is an online examination platform built specifically for students preparing for the Assam Direct Recruitment Examination (ADRE), Assam Police examinations, and other competitive exams. Our platform provides subject-wise mock tests, daily automated examinations, comprehensive question banks, and detailed performance tracking — all designed to help you prepare more effectively.

Every subject includes a Forever Free mock test, so you can start practising without any cost. For deeper practice, our daily mock tests and premium subject-wise tests are available through flexible subscription plans. All questions include detailed explanations in English and Assamese, helping you understand the reasoning behind every answer.
