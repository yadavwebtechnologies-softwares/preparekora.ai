FAQs

How do I upgrade my plan?

Go to the Purchase tab, select a plan, and complete the WhatsApp flow. Our team verifies your payment and activates the plan.

How many daily tests can I attempt?

Free: 1 per slot · Basic: 2 per slot · Standard: 3 per slot · Premium: all available tests.

Can I access paid homepage tests on Free plan?

No. Paid homepage tests require Standard or Premium plan. Forever Free tests in each subject are always available.
