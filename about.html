About

Learn about PrepareKora and why we built it.

PrepareKora is an online examination platform built specifically for students preparing for the Assam Direct Recruitment Examination (ADRE), Assam Police examinations, and other competitive exams. Our goal is simple: make quality practice material affordable and accessible to every aspirant in Assam.

What we offer

  • Subject-wise mock tests for ADRE, Assam Police, and general competitive exams.
  • A Forever Free mock test for every subject — no login required.
  • Daily automated mock tests at 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM IST.
  • Detailed result reviews with question-wise explanations in English and Assamese.
  • Performance tracking to help you focus on weaker areas.

Why we built PrepareKora

Many students in Assam struggle to find reliable, affordable practice material. Most platforms are either priced out of reach for daily practice, or they don't support regional languages. PrepareKora was built to solve both problems — practice should be cheap, tests should be frequent, and explanations should be available in the language you think in.

Our approach

Every question is curated from standard competitive exam patterns. Every explanation is reviewed before it's used in a published test. We don't try to be everything to everyone — we focus on what actually helps you score higher on the exam day.
