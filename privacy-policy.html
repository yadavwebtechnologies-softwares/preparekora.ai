Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary: We collect only what's necessary to operate PrepareKora — your name, email, and test activity. We do not sell your data. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (hashed, handled by Firebase Authentication).
  • Test activity: which tests you attempt, your answers, scores, and timestamps.
  • Subscription records: plan, activation, and expiry dates.
  • Basic technical data: IP address and browser information, used for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To deliver test content, score your attempts, and show you results.
  • To enforce daily-test quotas and subscription benefits.
  • To notify you about tests, subscriptions, and important platform updates.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse.

3. Data storage

Your data is stored on Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud) and accessed through Cloudflare Workers. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties except where necessary to run the platform (Firebase, Cloudflare) or where legally required.

5. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings tab of your dashboard. When you do:

  • Your profile, test history, and subscription records are removed or anonymised.
  • Financial records (like verified payments) may be retained for legal and accounting reasons.
  • Deletion cannot be undone.

6. Data retention

We retain account data while your account is active. After deletion, some records may be retained for compliance, dispute resolution, or fraud prevention for a limited period.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data — encrypted transport, secure authentication, and restricted server-side access. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

PrepareKora is designed for students and adults preparing for competitive exams. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us.

9. Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced on the platform.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please reach out via our Find Us page.
